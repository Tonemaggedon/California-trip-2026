Day 4
Tuesday 4 August ✓ BOOKED
🚶 No driving — on foot, bike & free shuttle

Today's Plan

  • Sunrise: Mirror Lake or Valley View before crowds. The light on Half Dome at dawn is one of the great natural spectacles on Earth.
  • Morning hike: Valley Floor Trail — gentle 13-mile loop (or shorter sections): El Capitan Meadow, Yosemite Falls base, Sentinel Beach. 📍 Falls trailhead
  • Yosemite Falls: Tallest waterfall in N. America at 2,425 feet. Lower falls trail (easy, 1 hour) puts you at the roaring base.
  • Afternoon: Rent bikes from Yosemite Valley Lodge — the best way to cover ground with the most scenery and least effort.
  • Half Dome views: Glacier Point for the classic Half Dome profile — outstanding photography, no strenuous hiking required.
  • Evening: Fire pits and stargazing at Autocamp. Yosemite is one of the darkest-sky locations in California.
🌟 Arrive at Valley attractions before 9am to beat the crowds. Valley shuttle is free and goes everywhere.
🐻 Wildlife: Black bears are present — make noise on trails, never store food in the car, use bear boxes at Autocamp overnight. Rattlesnakes possible on sun-warmed rocks; watch where you step. Mountain lions exist but are almost never seen. Millions hike here every year safely — be sensible and you'll be absolutely fine. NPS bear safety →

Top Travel Tips

🌫️ August in SF = Fog

San Francisco summers are famously cool and foggy — locals call it "Fogust." Pack a light jacket. The fog burns off most days by midday. Once you leave the city and head south, the coast warms up considerably.

⛽ Fuel Before Big Sur

Fill the tank in Santa Barbara before heading north. There is one petrol station in Big Sur charging roughly double the going rate. Fill up again in Pismo Beach or San Simeon before entering the Big Sur stretch.

📱 Download Offline Maps

Big Sur has almost no mobile signal. Download the offline map for the Hwy 1 Big Sur section the night before in Cambria. You'll need it for finding Pfeiffer Beach's unsigned turn especially.

🎫 America the Beautiful Pass

Your pass ($80/vehicle) covers Yosemite entrance fee and Pfeiffer Beach. It does not cover California State Parks (McWay Falls) or 17-Mile Drive ($12, private). Autocamp booking covers your timed entry — no separate permit needed.

🚗 Return the Car the Night Before

Return the hire car at SFO rental centre on the evening of Day 9. This is the single most stress-reducing thing you can do for an 08:20 departure. The AirTrain from the rental centre to the terminal takes 5 minutes.

💳 Tipping Culture

18–22% at restaurants, $2–5/day hotel housekeeping, 15–20% for Ubers. Budget roughly $50/day extra per couple for tips and incidentals.

🤖 Try a Waymo Driverless Taxi

Download the Waymo One app before you travel. Try it on the ride back from Alcatraz on Day 2 — you'll be talking about it for weeks.

🛣️ Highway 1 Conditions

Check roads.dot.ca.gov before the Day 8 PCH drive. August is usually fine but check the week before — sections occasionally close after winter storms.
